02 / Introducing CHAMP

Learning how the whole body moves.
Deciding how it should act.

Moving and manipulating are tightly connected. The body brings the hands into reach; the demands of a task shape the body's motion. CHAMP learns these relationships from heterogeneous human and robot motion, then transfers them to coordinated humanoid control.

01

Shared motion priors

Body joints, wrists, fingertips, and root motion provide a shared representation across humans and robot embodiments.

02

Adaptive feature access

The Coordination-oriented Action Expert selects source layers and projection experts for each input token.

03

Coordinated control

A flow-matching action model jointly generates whole-body motion latents and commands for both hands.

03 / Method

From motion prediction to robot action.

CHAMP first learns whole-body motion priors from heterogeneous human and robot data, then grounds these priors in robot actions and fine-tunes the action expert for each task.

Predict future body and hand motion.

The vision-language model uses RGB image input, an instruction prompt, and pose information to predict future body and hand motion. Body and hand motion are encoded separately.
